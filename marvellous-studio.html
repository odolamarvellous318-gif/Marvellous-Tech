About the studio

Built for ideas that deserve to move.

Marvellous Studio is a multidisciplinary creative digital studio founded by Odola Temidayo Marvellous, Founder & Creative Director.

We bring together creative design, branding, web design and development, Shopify e-commerce, copywriting, book writing and publishing, and data analytics to create solutions that are both visually engaging and useful.

Rather than treating each project as an isolated task, we look at the bigger picture — how the brand communicates, how customers experience it, and how the final product supports the next move.
