# Blessed Agencia Reference — Design Notes

The reference uses a dark near-black canvas with a pale chartreuse accent, warm off-white typography, oversized expressive serif headlines, compact uppercase sans-serif labels, and thin hairline rules. The header is minimal and horizontal, with a small wordmark on the left, anchor links across the top, and a bright rounded booking CTA on the right.

The homepage is a long editorial one-page agency site. It begins with an asymmetric hero: a large serif headline and short positioning paragraph on the left, a framed project/statement card on the right, and a concise proof strip below with three metrics. A narrow horizontal phrase band separates major sections. Services are presented as numbered editorial rows/cards with plain-language descriptions and direct scope links. Pricing is organized through category tabs and tiered cards. A compact FAQ uses native accordion rows, followed by a calm inquiry form with only the fields needed to start a useful conversation.

The reference has restrained motion: reveal-on-scroll entrances, subtle hover states, a continuously moving tools/platform strip, and rounded booking/panel elements used selectively. Its strongest differentiators are typography, editorial rhythm, high-contrast lime accent, simple numbered structure, generous spacing, and the clear progression from positioning to services to pricing to FAQ to contact.

For Marvellous Studio, preserve the user's supplied orange accent and logo, real Shopify projects, book-work samples, pricing, currency selector, and contact flow, while translating the layout into a single cohesive agency-style landing experience with anchor navigation and an editorial serif display voice.
